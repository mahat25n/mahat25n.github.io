Atlas of Scent

A perfume can bring several places into the same bottle. This map starts with five places associated with distinct scents, from May rose in Grasse to vanilla in Madagascar’s Sava region.

The data behind the map

I placed five reference points in QGIS. The map pairs Grasse, France, with May rose, Reggio Calabria, Italy, with bergamot, Dhofar, Oman, with frankincense, Kannauj, India, with mitti attar, and Sava, Madagascar, with vanilla. I used Blender to render the map and draw the coloured trails. The points refer to the named places or regions. The trails are illustrative.

Scent and place

The five examples connect scent to different kinds of places, from cities associated with perfumery to regions associated with cultivation and harvesting. Each point gives one geographic reference for the scent. The map represents five selected associations and does not estimate where production is concentrated or how fragrances move through trade.
