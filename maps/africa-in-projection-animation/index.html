Africa in Projection (animation)

The animation begins with a view of Africa’s elevation and ends with twelve maps side by side. I let each view take shape with its projection name in sight. Watch the relief change from highlands and drainage basins to city populations, coastal lighthouses and the distribution of solar radiation and wind.

How to read the animation

I prepared the geographic layers in QGIS and rendered the relief in Blender. Each scene pairs a named projection with a different feature. The camera angle and surface height help bring that feature into view. The final top-down comparison makes it easier to see each projected outline. Open the full atlas sheet.

Economic notes

What does proximity to the coast do for a firm that still needs a reliable way to reach port? How does the timing of rain change the value of a loan? Each scene links to a longer analysis of the decisions behind the pattern, along with the data and projection used for the map.
