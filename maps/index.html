The Red Sea shift

Animated story

Global markets after the tariff announcement

How did selected stock markets move around the April 2025 tariff announcement? This animation follows local closing index levels relative to 2 April. Coral marks a fall and teal a rise. The timeline is descriptive: it does not isolate the effect of tariffs from everything else happening in markets.

Day 11 · Senses: Sound

New York City: nighttime noise reports

Where does New York report noise after dark? I grouped NYC 311 noise reports from July 2025, between 11 pm and 7 am, into hexagons. Purple marks fewer reports; pink, orange and yellow mark more. These are complaints, not measured sound levels.

Day 10 · Prompting tool

Where Africa is splitting apart

A continent viewed through its relief and plate boundaries. The East African Rift and the Red Sea stand out against the surrounding landscape. Elevation and bathymetry provide the setting; the marked boundaries come from a tectonic plate model.

Day 09 · Urban / Rural

Where people live in Türkiye

Population density across Türkiye, with a closer view of İstanbul. The shaded surface represents estimated population concentration, not terrain elevation.

Day 08 · Utopia

The wind that could power Somalia

A different way to look at Somalia’s landscape: through its wind. The colours show modelled mean wind speed at 100 metres above ground. They help reveal the resource, but do not tell us where a wind farm would be feasible or how much electricity it would produce.

Day 07 · 10-minute map

Where land lies opposite land

What lies on the other side of the Earth? I overlaid land with its antipodes. Orange marks places where land has land directly opposite it, pairing distant regions on the same globe.

Day 06 · Vintage

The age of the world’s temperature records

When do the available temperature records begin? Colours show the first year with usable monthly data at each station in NOAA’s GHCN-M v4 archive.

Day 05 · Senses: Sight

How clear is the ocean?

A view of the ocean through the light it absorbs and scatters. Lower Kd490 values mean light penetrates farther into the water. These satellite estimates show light attenuation, not a direct measure of water quality.

Day 04 · Clusters

Where submarine cables come ashore

The internet has a coastal geography. I used DBSCAN to group nearby cable landing points. Colours show the number of landing points in each cluster; grey points fall outside the clusters. The source includes existing and planned infrastructure at approximate locations.

Day 03 · Polygons

What Africa exports

One leading export gives each country its colour. This map compares the highest-value HS four-digit export products in 2024, excluding aircraft and ships. It is a view of leading products, not the whole export basket.

Day 02 · Lines

The routes that connect the world

The oceans look open, but shipping follows remarkably concentrated paths. Brighter lines show denser traffic; the closer view brings the Red Sea, Gulf of Aden and Strait of Hormuz into focus.

Day 01 · Points

Refugee camps in sub-Saharan Africa

A point on this map can represent decades of displacement. I mapped camp sites recorded between 1999 and 2024, with circle size showing years in operation and colour distinguishing sites open or closed at the end of 2024.
