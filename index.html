About

Dr. Mahat is an Assistant Professor of Economics at Ibn Haldun University in Istanbul. His research interests include international trade, financial economics, urban economics and development economics.

His recent work examines financial market forecasting, regional poverty and household vulnerability to economic shocks. His current projects focus on occupational mobility, inflation forecasting and economic behaviour in large language models.
